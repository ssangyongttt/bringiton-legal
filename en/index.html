Privacy Policy

BringItOn Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026 · Version 1.8 · 한국어

BringItOn (the “Service”) treats your personal data with care and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable law. You can use the Service with a nickname alone, without signing up, and in that case we collect no information that directly identifies you — no name, phone number or email address.
Kakao sign-in is optional, and only if you choose it do we receive your Kakao member number. We do not receive your name, email, phone number or profile picture.
Play Games linking (Android) is also optional, and even if you link, the Service stores nothing.

About this translation This English text is provided for your convenience. The Service is operated from the Republic of Korea and the Korean version is the governing text; if the two differ, the Korean version controls. You can read it at the Korean policy page.

We do not request and do not receive your email address, real name, profile picture or friend list. The Google sign-in step itself is provided by Google, and the handling of personal data during that step is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

02Why we process it

  • Running games — creating and joining rooms, synchronising rounds in real time, deciding outcomes and showing results
  • Telling users apart — distinguishing the people in a room and showing their results
  • Linking an account — if you choose Kakao sign-in, connecting your Kakao member number to your anonymous identifier so that you carry on as the same user after changing device or reinstalling
  • Showing your records — providing your own history, titles and per-game records
  • Operating the Service — blocking abuse and abnormal access, analysing the causes of errors, and improving the Service
  • Usage analysis — statistically analysing which screens and features are used, in order to improve the Service. We do not identify or track individual users, and use the data only in aggregate.
  • Serving advertising — showing in-app advertising and measuring its performance
  • Verifying purchases — checking whether “Remove ads” was purchased so that advertising is not shown, and preventing one receipt being applied to several users
  • Answering enquiries — receiving, reviewing and answering your enquiry, and finding the cause of the problem you raised. The app version and device details sent with an enquiry are used only to reproduce and confirm the problem.
  • Sending notifications — where you have consented, sending room invitations, friend requests, game results and announcements

04Disclosure to third parties

The Service does not provide your personal data to third parties, except:

  • where you have consented in advance;
  • where required by law, or requested by an investigative authority following lawful procedure.
Checking a purchase with the store When you buy “Remove ads”, the Service sends the receipt number issued by the store back to that store (Google LLC / Apple Inc.) to confirm the purchase is genuine. No information that could identify you is sent. Because this returns a value the store itself issued, it does not constitute disclosure of personal data to a third party.
Receiving something from Kakao is not disclosure If you choose Kakao sign-in, the Service receives your Kakao member number through Kakao’s own consent flow. That is the Service receiving information; there is no case in which the Service provides your information to a third party, Kakao included.

07Advertising and push notifications

Advertising

The Service shows one full-screen advertisement only, when you leave after finishing a full session. We do not show advertising during a game or between rounds, we do not use banner advertising at the bottom of the screen, and we do not use rewarded video advertising.

If this is your first time, no advertisement is shown at the end of your first session.

We use your device’s advertising identifier to serve advertising, and you can restrict this yourself:

  • Android — Settings → Google → Ads → Delete or reset advertising ID
  • iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

Restricting the advertising identifier places no limit on playing; it only makes the advertising you see less relevant.

Friends

Once you are friends, the other person continues to see your nickname and avatar. They stop seeing them when the friendship ends.

Tapping a friend invite link does not by itself create a friendship. The link opens an “add friend” screen, and the friendship is created only after you accept it there yourself. An invite link stops opening 7 days after it is created.

Ending a friendship removes it from both lists at once, and the other person is not told. Friend invites from a user you have blocked do not open.

When a friend calls you into a room, a record of the call is kept and is deleted 24 hours later. That record is used only to send the notification and to stop the same person calling you repeatedly in a short space of time.

Public rooms

A public room is one anyone can enter without an invitation. If you enter one, your nickname and avatar are visible to users you do not know.

Public rooms have no room name. The list shows only the chosen game and the number of players; there is no free text written by users.

If you would rather not enter public rooms, use only rooms created with an invite code. An invited room can be entered only by someone who knows the code.

Reporting and blocking

You can report another user whose nickname is inappropriate, and you can block a particular user so that you no longer see them. A report record keeps the anonymous identifiers of the reporter and the reported user, and is used only to filter out duplicate reports.

Where several different users report the same nickname, the Company may stop that nickname from being used any further. In that case the Company does not erase a nickname a user has already chosen.

The server keeps only the time of that action. The Company does not access your device storage directly.

Running “Delete my data” also deletes the reports you made. Reports that other users made about you belong to those users and therefore remain, but the value identifying you is removed from them.

Push notifications

Push notifications are sent only where you have consented, and a push registration token is issued when you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

There are two kinds of notification, different in nature, and consent is taken separately for each.

TypeWhat it isDefault
Service notificationsSent when a friend of yours calls you into a room. Someone you have not added as a friend cannot send one.On
Marketing notificationsNews and event announcements. Under the Korean Network Act, the sender’s name and contact details are shown with them.Off (only if you consent)

Service notifications are not advertising, so they are managed separately from consent to marketing notifications. Turning off one still leaves you receiving the other; turning off both deletes your push registration token.

  • In the app — Settings → Notification settings, either all at once or by type (room invitations / friend requests / results and titles / announcements and events).
  • On your device — block them entirely in your operating system’s per-app notification settings.

Refusing notifications places no limit on playing. Note only that if you turn off room-invitation notifications, you will not be told when a friend calls you.

Usage analysis

To improve the Service we collect statistics on which screens and features are used. The purpose is limited to statistical analysis; we do not identify individual users and do not track your activity on other apps or websites.

08Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop its processing.

Doing it yourself in the app

  • Change nickname or avatar — Settings → Edit profile
  • Delete all data — Settings → Delete my data. Your anonymous authentication identifier and the play records linked to it, your Kakao member number (if you signed in), your friendships, the friend invite links you created, records of calling friends, your block list, the reports you made, your push registration token and notification consent history, your nickname reset history, your enquiries, your usage analytics records and your “Remove ads” purchase record are all deleted immediately and cannot be recovered afterwards. If you signed in with Kakao, the Kakao account link is also released.
  • Deleting the app — deleting the app from your device does not itself delete what is stored on the server; that follows the periods in section 3 above. If you signed in with Kakao it is not deleted automatically, so please use “Delete my data” before deleting the app. Records stored only on the device disappear when you delete the app.

By contacting us

You can make a request through the contact details in section 13 below.

Some things remain after deletion “Delete my data” erases your information; records left by other users are their information and are not deleted.
  • A record that another user blocked you — that is their choice, and it stays.
  • A record that another user reported you — it belongs to the reporter and stays, but the value identifying you is deleted from it so that you cannot be recognised.
  • The “Remove ads” purchase itself — the purchase record is deleted, but the purchase remains with the store. Use “Restore purchase” in Settings with the same store account to apply it again. (The purchase record must be deleted for the receipt fingerprint to go with it, so that restoring works properly.)
  • A copy of an enquiry already delivered — an enquiry you send is delivered to the operator when it is received (see section 6). The enquiry stored in the Service’s database is deleted, but a copy already delivered and sitting in the operator’s messenger is not deleted by this feature. If you want that copy deleted, please ask through the contact details in section 13.
You cannot delete during a game Erasing a participant’s information while a game is running throws off the results for everyone else playing. Please delete after the game has ended.

Note that because the Service holds no sign-up information, we cannot verify your identity; to act on a request, you must include the anonymous identifier shown on the app’s settings screen.

09Destruction of personal data

  • Procedure — personal data whose retention period has passed, or whose purpose of processing has been achieved, is destroyed by an automatic deletion job without a separate approval step.
  • Method — information held as electronic files is permanently erased by technical means that make it impossible to recover or reconstruct.

10Security measures

  • Encryption in transit — all communication between app and server is encrypted with TLS.
  • Access control — database row level security (RLS) policies restrict users from reaching data that is not their own.
  • Minimal collection — we collect only the minimum needed to provide the Service, and collect no directly identifying information.
  • Access logging — access records for the personal-data processing system are retained and reviewed.

11Children under 14

Under the Personal Information Protection Act, processing the personal data of a child under 14 requires the consent of a legal guardian, and because the Service has no sign-up procedure it cannot verify such consent. Children under 14 therefore may not use Kakao sign-in.

Without signing in, we receive no personal data Every feature of the Service works without signing in, and in that case we collect no personal data that directly identifies a user. Children under 14 can use the Service without signing in.

If we find that personal data of a child under 14 has been collected, we destroy it without delay.

12Changes to this policy

This policy may be amended as the law or the Service changes. Changes are posted in the in-app notices on the day they take effect.

However, we give notice from 30 days before a change takes effect where it:

  • adds an item to what we collect;
  • lengthens a retention period;
  • adds a disclosure to a third party, a processor, or a transfer outside Korea;
  • gives the Company a new power to change a user’s information or settings.
Why this does not say “significant changes” If the Company decides case by case what counts as significant, the test moves with whoever is deciding. The four cases above can be checked by anyone by comparing this policy’s tables with the previous version.
